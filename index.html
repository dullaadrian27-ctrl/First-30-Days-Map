The Missed Touchpoint Recovery Rule

Systems break. What separates a turnkey company is what happens next. When a touchpoint gets missed:

  1. The person in the "who notices" column catches it within 24 hours.
  2. The owner completes it the same day, late beats never.
  3. If a family felt the miss, leadership acknowledges it directly. No excuses, just ownership.
  4. You fix the trigger, not just the person. Ask: why didn't the system remind them?

A turnkey company isn't one where good things happen because you have good people. It's one where you've defined what good looks like, assigned who owns it, built the trigger and built accountability around whether it happened.

Part 7

The 30-Day Experience Check-In™

"Everything okay?" is useless. Give your director a structured 5-minute conversation. Practice it here, then use the same six questions with every family on Day 30.

Why question 3 matters most

You are literally asking: is there a gap between what you expected and what you experienced? That is the Marketing-to-Experience Gap™, caught in real time, while it is still a gap and not an exit.

Route the family

Most programs stop at the check-in. The money is in what you do next. A happy family on Day 30 is your best referral source. A family with a gap is a withdrawal notice you still have time to cancel.

The Advocate Path

Within 48 hours, while the feeling is fresh:

  • Ask for a Google review, using their own words from question 2.
  • Make the referral ask specific: "Who else do you know with a little one?"
  • Invite them to be a parent voice at your next tour or open house.
